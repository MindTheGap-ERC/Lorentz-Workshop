# Breakout Group - Data Limitations

In the breakout group about data limitations, we just discussed problems. The following are the problems we came up with:

- High frequency of missing character data
    - When is it a problem, and how much
    - taphonomic controls as drivers of patterns of missing data
    - log taphonomic information alongside characters, test for correlation with missing data.
- How important are different morphological characters in phylogenetic analyses?
    - relationship with decision to code non-convergent traits (correct assumption?)
    - weighting characters (type of prior?)
    - Could we infer weights?
  - How do choices about the number of characters on different parts of morphology influence this?
- Can we infer different speciation modes?
    - How much data does speciation require (size of matrix, length of ranges)
    - What do the modes really mean (ancestors vs descendants, ecological realism)
- How much age uncertainty is permitted to still enable inference (accurately)?
- Non-uniform age uncertainty
    - How do they interact
    - sensible prior shapes?
- Modeling lithology in terrestrial environments
